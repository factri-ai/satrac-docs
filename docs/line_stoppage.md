## 1. Introduction
This document outlines the guidelines for using the DataWiz application in the Bangalore and Chennai plants for the Line Stoppage Trackings.

---

## 2. Installation and Login
The application URL is [https://satrac-line-tracker.datawiz.app/login](https://satrac-line-tracker.datawiz.app/login). Chrome is the recommended browser.

### Mobile App Installation
Using the mobile app is recommended for making work order confirmations.

1. Open the application URL in your mobile browser.
2. Click on the **settings** button from the top right corner.
3. Click on the **‘Add to Home Screen’** button.
4. Select the **‘Install’** option.
5. Login using the credentials provided by the DataWiz team.

## 3. Scan the Line QR Code
The Line QR Codes will be provided by the DataWiz team

1. Login to the app on a **Mobile** device.
2. At the landing page, you will see a **SCAN QR/BARCODE** button. Click on it to scan the respective line QR code. Alternatively, you can also select the respective line from the dropdown provided below.
3. You will be taken to the **Lines** page, where all the stoppages, active and resolved, will be accessiable.

### a. Report a Stoppage 

1. At the **Lines** page, press the **Report a Stoppage** button from the top section.
2. After clicking on the button, you will taken to the stoppage reporting form. Here you can enter all the stoppage instance related information.
    - Select if the stoppage is **Blocking** or **Non-Blocking** in nature.
    - Select the reason category and code most relevant for this instance of stoppage from the dropdown.
    - Update the start time of the stoppage if required.
    - Enter the SF number this instance of stoppage is having effect on. You can scan the SF number QR code as well to auto fill the number directly.
    - Enter the notes for your side adding more context to the stoppage reporting such that your team member is informed correctly.
3. After entering all the stoppage related details, press the Report Stoppage button.
4. This will report the stoppage, and the escalation email chain will be triggere. Please refer the notification section for more details.

### b. Discuss Over the Stoppage Issue

1. Your team can dicuss over the specific issue directly at the stoppage details page itsel.
2. Click on the **Message** tab from the top section. It will open a chat window, where team members can send their messages, tag the relevant users.
3. Real-time notifications will be sent to the tagged users.

### c. Resolve a Stoppage
1. At the **Lines** page, select the stoppage that needs to be resolved.
2. Click on the **Resolve Stoppage** button. Enter the resolution related notes and change the resolution time i.e **End Time** if required.
3. Click on the Resolve button.
4. This will report the stoppage resolution and the resolution related mail will be sent to all the relevant groups.

## 4. Access Past Stoppages

1. For reference or analysis to access all the past stoppages click on **View All Stoppages** button from the bottom of the landing page.
2. Based on the plant assess, the user will see all the past stoppages.
3. Use the provided filter options to look for the stoppages required for analysis.
